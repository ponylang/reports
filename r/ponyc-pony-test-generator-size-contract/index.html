Six properties replace 37 examples and exercise the size and content of 22 pony_test generator families.

Motivation

Fixed boundary, duplicate, and retry cases run every time. Varying the seed exercises additional sizes and contents. The properties preserve the bounds and identity cases covered by the removed examples.
